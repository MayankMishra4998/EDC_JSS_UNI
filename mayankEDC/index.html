Steve Jobs: The Visionary Architect

"At the Intersection of Technology & The Liberal Arts"

        Steve Jobs revolutionized six global industries: personal computing, animated motion pictures, digital music, mobile phones, tablet computing, and digital typography. Co-founding Apple in a Silicon Valley garage in 1976, he championed the belief that technology must be intuitively human, aesthetically immaculate, and engineered with soul.

"Design is not just what it looks like and feels like. Design is how it works."
